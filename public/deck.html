She can make a phone call. She will never set up a wallet.

Wallets are appsSeed phrases, browser extensions, gas. Most of the people we would like to send money to stop at step one.
Phones are where the scams areMalaysia alone lost RM 2.97 billion to online scams in 2025, up from RM 1.57 billion the year before (Home Ministry figures). An AI that can move money on a call has to be impossible to talk into it.
So the AI gets no say over the moneyEvery phone number gets its own NEAR contract, owned by the person or their family. The contract holds the limits. The AI can only ask.

One call in, one packet out

Say the words. Get paid.

A money packet is the Chinese red packet made digital: one gift, split between a group, equal or lucky. The lucky split is rolled by the contract, so one person pulls most of it and another pulls dust.

The agent that talks to the opener never knows the words. Code compares them with what the caller said, and a second model call in the TEE judges by sound when speech-to-text spells them differently.

The AI only asks. The contract decides.

Up to 0.2 NEAR or 10 USDC, saved recipientA spoken 4-digit PIN. Three wrong tries lock the line for 15 minutes.
More than that, inside the limitsThe PIN, then an Approve / Cancel / Not me tap on WhatsApp. “Not me” locks the AI out for 15 minutes.
Over a limitNot sent. The contract refuses it whatever the AI says.
First payment to a new accountHeld by the contract for a few minutes, then sent. Saying “cancel” stops it. A scammer cannot get money out in a hurry.
Worst case, with a bad operatorThe daily limit, to saved recipients only. The owner ends it with one transaction.

What NEAR does here

A wallet for every phone numberA factory creates one per number. The wallet code is published once as a global contract, so a new wallet costs 0.013 NEAR.
The rules, in a contractPer-send and per-day limits for NEAR and USDC, saved recipients, a wait for new ones, freeze, owner-only withdraw.
The packets contractFund, split from the block’s random seed, one share per account, refund after expiry.
Private AI with proofChat and speech-to-text run on NEAR AI Cloud inside TEEs. Our server verifies the Intel and NVIDIA attestation and shows it on /proof.
Names you can say out loud“liho dot near” is read back before anything moves.

Who pays

Brands and communities in the contractThey fund packets as promotions: “say our slogan, get paid”. The contract takes 5% plus the cost of each new wallet when the packet is funded. Family packets are free.
Every packet makes walletsOpening a share gives you a wallet tied to your phone number with money in it. Red packets signed up an estimated 100 to 200 million bank cards for WeChat Pay in one 2015 holiday.
Packets that grow next, not builtA packet staked until it is opened. The opener gets the gift plus the staking yield; Ringfence keeps a share of the yield.
Cash-out and swap fees laterA small fee when money leaves through NEAR Intents.
